
# CodeAlpha Internship – Task 4: Technical Indicators Case Study

## Project Overview
This project is a case study completed as part of the CodeAlpha Stock Market & Trading Basics Internship. It explores commonly used technical indicators in stock market analysis and explains how traders use them to study price trends, momentum, and potential trading opportunities.

## Objectives
- Understand moving averages and their applications.
- Learn the fundamentals of the Simple Moving Average (SMA) and Exponential Moving Average (EMA).
- Understand the Relative Strength Index (RSI) and Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD).
- Explore how technical indicators can support trading decisions.
- Learn the importance of risk management, backtesting, and paper trading.

## Topics Covered
1. Introduction to Technical Analysis
2. Simple Moving Average (SMA)
3. Exponential Moving Average (EMA)
4. Relative Strength Index (RSI)
5. Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD)
6. Trading Strategy Framework
7. Risk Management and Position Sizing
8. Backtesting and Paper Trading
9. Conclusion and References

## Deliverable
**File:** `Task4_Technical_Indicators_Case_Study.pdf`

The report includes explanations, illustrative charts, a strategy framework, risk management concepts, and reference links.
